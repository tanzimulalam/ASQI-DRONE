# Outer-arc prop guard for the X500 V2 (10 inch props)

`propguard-outer-x500-10in.stl` is one guard. **Print four.** Generated by
`scripts/propguard_outer.py`; change a parameter at the top and re-run to regenerate.

This replaces `propguard-x500-10in.stl` (the full ring). Do not fly the full ring:
its mounting posts stand inside the blade circle, 95 to 115 mm from the shaft,
and reach 11 mm above the bottom of the blades, so the props would hit them.
Its inboard half also fouled the frame mounted on top of the Pixhawk.

## What changed

- The guard only covers the **outboard half** of each propeller (a half circle,
  inner radius 137 mm, 10 mm outside the blade tips).
- Everything inside the blade circle is **struts at arm-tube height**, about 30 mm
  under the blades. The only parts that rise to blade height (four posts and the
  arc) are outside the blade tips. The generator script checks this and refuses
  to write the file if anything intrudes.
- At blade height, the nearest part of the guard is about **286 mm from the
  airframe centre** (the full ring came to about 111 mm).

## Check these first, on one test print

1. **Clamp position.** The clamp sits **55 to 85 mm inboard of the motor shaft**
   (`CLAMP_X = -70`). That section of tube must be bare, clear of the motor mount.
2. **Clamp fit.** Snaps up over the **16 mm** tube from underneath, 260 degree
   wrap, 0.2 mm clearance.
3. **Prop height.** Still the unmeasured assumption: blades **34 mm above the top
   of the arm tube** (`PROP_H`). The band spans 4 mm below to 11 mm above the
   blade bottom.

## Dimensions

| | |
|---|---|
| Arc | half circle, -90 to +90 degrees from outboard, 2 mm wall, 15 mm tall, 5 mm lip on top |
| Footprint | 229 x 288 x 59 mm |
| Mass | about 48 g in PLA each, about 191 g for four |

## Printing

- The STL is **already flipped**: lip flat on the bed, clamp pointing up. Do not rotate it.
- **Supports:** on, tree, touching build plate only (needed under the struts and clamp).
- **Walls:** 5. **Infill:** 20 percent. **Layer height:** 0.2 to 0.28 mm.
- PETG survives strikes better; PLA is fine for a bumper.

## Fitting

Snap the clamp up onto the tube with the arc outboard, centred on the motor.
Add one zip tie around the clamp. Spin each prop by hand and check nothing
touches, then re-check level hover.
