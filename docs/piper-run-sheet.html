Piper: indoor showcase run sheet

Ground station on drone-tx · cockpit at http://10.42.0.254:8000

1. Power up

  1. Battery in. Ground station on. Both start everything themselves.
  2. Laptop joins Wi-Fi drone-tx.
  3. Open http://10.42.0.254:8000. If the page is stale, hard reload with Ctrl+Shift+R.
  4. Piper's card should read ONLINE with a battery around 16 V. Press Fly Piper and enter the aircraft password.

2. Before arming

  • Transmitter on, throttle fully down, SwC middle (Alt Hold).
  • Hardware safety switch pressed until solid.
  • Cockpit shows video and detection boxes. Click the page once so the keyboard works.
  • If the aircraft refuses to arm, the cockpit says why in an amber CANNOT ARM band with the fix.

3. Flying

KeyWhat it does
W / SThrottle up / down. Release = hold height.
A / DYaw left / right.
Numpad 8246Pitch forward/back, roll left/right. Arrows work too.
1…6Fly to and hold 0.5, 0.8, 1.1, 1.4, 1.7, 2.1 m.
`Land. The flight controller lands itself.

Heights are measured from where you armed. Touching W or S cancels a hold and gives you the throttle back.

4. Emergency

If anything looks wrong
Flip SwC UP. The aircraft lands itself and the ground station stops sending sticks.
If it tips or hits something: throttle fully down and Disarm. Do not try to catch it with the sticks.
SwC DOWN gives you manual control in Stabilize, where the throttle stick directly drives the motors.

5. First flight of the session

  1. Hop: lift to 20–30 cm, hold two seconds, set down. If it leans or skates, stop and investigate.
  2. Lift decisively to about a metre. Do not creep: tip-overs happen with a leg still loaded.
  3. Check gently: left, right, forward, back, yaw each way. Each should go where you expect.
  4. Try 2, then 4, then ` so the height keys are familiar before an audience sees them.
  5. Practise SwC up once in the air.

6. If something refuses

Message or symptomDo this
Hardware safety switchPress and hold the button on the aircraft until its light is solid.
Pitch / roll not neutralHands off both sets of sticks, then arm again.
Battery below minimumFit a charged pack. Arming needs above 14.0 V.
RC not foundTransmitter on, throttle down. Check the receiver LED is solid.
Needs position / fenceShould not appear indoors now. If it does, the aircraft is not in indoor configuration.
Video black, detection still workingHard reload the page. The stream drops and does not retry.
Cockpit says CONTROL LOST or NO CONTROL AUTHORITYLand with SwC up. Do not keep flying.
Aircraft drifts steadily one wayLand. Check the battery has not shifted and the props are clean.

7. Between flights

  • Swap to a charged pack: arming stops below 14.0 V, and the aircraft hovers at about 68% throttle, so it is not a light airframe.
  • Touch each motor. One noticeably hotter than the rest means stop flying and tell me.
  • Check the props for chips after any contact.

8. After the showcase

Restore before this aircraft flies outdoors
It is currently in indoor configuration: no geofence, GPS pre-arm checks dropped, and the mode switch remapped. 
ssh -t drone "python3 /tmp/piper_outdoor.py"
Both values it reads back must be 1, and the switch goes back to Stabilize / Alt Hold / Loiter.
